Privacy Policy

How Ant’s Jets handles quote enquiries and customer contact details.

Information we collect

When you request a quote, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, location and details about the cleaning work you need.

How we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to your request, provide quotes, arrange work and improve our marketing/reporting.

Optional website analytics

Optional analytics is off by default. We ask you to choose whether to allow it, and respect browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. You can reject it or withdraw permission at any time using Analytics settings at the bottom of each public page. Your choice does not affect requesting a quote.

When configured and allowed, our self-hosted Umami analytics at analytics.antsjets.co.uk measures page visits, referring website, campaign labels, device/browser type and approximate location derived by the analytics server from connection information. It also counts clicks to call, text or WhatsApp and quote-form openings. A quote link click and a visible form are recorded separately; neither proves that an enquiry was submitted. Successful form submissions are not currently measured by this analytics integration.

We do not send enquiry messages, form fields, names, email addresses or telephone numbers to analytics. Page paths are recorded without general URL queries or fragments; only restricted campaign labels may accompany page views, and referrers are reduced to the referring website. Umami receives connection information including IP address and browser headers to provide aggregated statistics. Hosting access logs may also contain connection information. This is not a promise that no personal data is processed.

Umami does not set analytics cookies. Google Analytics is currently disabled. If configured later, it will load only after you allow analytics, may use first-party Google Analytics cookies, and process analytics data through Google. Advertising consent remains denied. Withdrawing analytics permission stops our analytics events and removes accessible first-party Google Analytics cookies. Your choice is stored in your browser for up to 180 days; if browser storage is unavailable, it applies only to the current page. The self-hosted service is not yet connected and sends nothing until configured and you opt in.

Quote form and contact services

Our embedded quote form is provided through GoHighLevel / LeadConnector. Details you send through it are used to handle your enquiry. That service is separate from optional website analytics and may process connection information when the form loads. The analytics controls above do not block the quote form or delete enquiry records. Contact us with questions or requests about your personal information.
